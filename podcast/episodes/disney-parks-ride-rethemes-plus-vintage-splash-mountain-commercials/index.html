About Dreaming of Disney

Dreaming of Disney is the Disney parks podcast from Main St. Dreamers, hosted by Tim and Audreyann. We talk Disneyland, trip planning, family experiences, park strategy, Disney news, and plenty of opinions along the way.

Main St. Dreamers is also home to our park-inspired merch and Park Scout, our Disneyland companion app.
