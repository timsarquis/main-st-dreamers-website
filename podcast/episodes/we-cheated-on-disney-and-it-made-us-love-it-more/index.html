The conversation

In This Episode

We finally did it. We went to a non-Disney theme park… and it completely changed how we see Disney.


After a family trip to San Diego and our first-ever visit to SeaWorld, we talk about the good, the confusing, and the expensive. From incredible animal encounters and empty rides to paid lockers, pricey food, and a roller coaster that nearly traumatized Tim, this day gave us a lot to think about.


Then we stopped at Disneyland on the way home and everything snapped into focus. Food prices, quality, crowd control, cast members, and the little details Disney gets right without you even noticing.


We also cover online reactions to our SeaWorld posts, a monorail breakdown evacuation we narrowly avoided, current Disneyland crowd vibes, and how we’re rethinking our last-day Disney tradition to end trips on a happier note.


If you’ve ever wondered whether Disney is really “worth it,” this episode might surprise you.
