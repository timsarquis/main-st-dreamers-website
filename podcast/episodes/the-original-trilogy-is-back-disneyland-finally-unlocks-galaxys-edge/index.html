In This Episode

Big Star Wars news just dropped, and Disneyland fans have been waiting years for this. Galaxy’s Edge is officially unlocking its timeline, bringing the Original Trilogy back into the land in a big way. Darth Vader, classic stormtroopers, original-era music, and major atmosphere changes are on the way… and yes, this is a Disneyland-only update (sorry, Disney World).

In this episode of Dreaming of Disney, Tim and Audreyann break down what’s changing, why fans pushed so hard for this, and how Disney is dividing Galaxy’s Edge into different Star Wars eras. We talk characters, music, Smuggler’s Run updates, Star Wars Nite timing, and whether this finally fixes one of the land’s biggest complaints.

If you’ve ever felt like Galaxy’s Edge was missing something… this might be the update you’ve been waiting for.
