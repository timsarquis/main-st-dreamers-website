The conversation

In This Episode

After years of wondering what Club 33 was really like, we finally got to experience Disneyland’s most exclusive club for ourselves… and it all started because of a message from someone in our TikTok live.


In this episode, we’re sharing the full story of how a listener helped us score reservations at Club 33 for our anniversary weekend, what it actually feels like walking through those hidden doors in New Orleans Square, and whether the experience lived up to the hype.


We talk about the nerves before dinner, the surprisingly emotional moments throughout the night, the unbelievable food (including the steak Tim is still thinking about days later), exclusive merch, watching Fantasmic! from the balcony, and the little details that made the night feel truly unforgettable.


Plus, we share some honest thoughts about the exclusivity of Club 33, what surprised us most, and why the kindness behind the invitation meant even more than the dinner itself.


It was magical, surreal, emotional… and probably the fanciest either of us will ever feel at Disneyland.
