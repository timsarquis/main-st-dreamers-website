In This Episode

We just got back from seeing Toy Story 5, and we have thoughts.


In this episode, we share our spoiler-filled reactions to Pixar’s latest sequel, why it works better than Toy Story 4, and the emotional moments that hit us the hardest. We also dive into the film’s biggest theme: technology, screen time, and whether kids are losing the magic of imaginative play.


Plus, our favorite scenes, the references you may have missed, why Woody will always be our favorite cowboy, and what we hope to see in Toy Story 6.


If you grew up with Toy Story and now have kids of your own, this conversation is for you.
